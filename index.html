Frequently asked questions

Before you decide

Can I study abroad without JAMB?

Often, yes. Many universities abroad admit students on WAEC or NECO results, sometimes after a one-year foundation programme. Requirements depend on the country, university and course, and we check them for you at your assessment.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the services you choose and the country. Our Academy courses and admissions support have separate fees, paid in stages. You get the full fee schedule and refund terms in writing before paying anything. University tuition and visa fees are paid directly to the university and embassy.

Do you guarantee admission or a visa?

No. No honest consultancy can. We make sure applications are complete, genuine and sent to universities that suit the student, which gives the best chance of success.

Is it all online? Can I meet you?

Yes. Classes and consultations run online, so students and families can work with us from any country. In-person meetings can be arranged on request.

Will a medical degree from abroad be recognised at home?

A foreign medical degree is only useful if the medical council in your home country recognises the school, for example the Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria. We check this with you before you apply to any medical programme.
